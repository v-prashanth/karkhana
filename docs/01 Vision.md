Executive Summary

Karkhana is not another CRM.

It is not another ERP.

It is not another website builder.

It is not another invoicing software.

Karkhana is a modular business platform that enables businesses of every size—from a one-person workshop to a growing enterprise—to establish their digital presence, manage daily operations, strengthen customer relationships, and grow their business from one unified workspace.

The philosophy behind Karkhana is simple:

Every business deserves enterprise-quality tools without enterprise complexity.

Core Philosophy

Everything we build must satisfy these principles.

Principle 1
Businesses come first.

Technology is secondary.

Customers don't buy software.

They buy solutions.

Principle 2
Simplicity over Features.

A thousand features nobody understands create confusion.

Ten features that save two hours every day create loyal customers.

Principle 3
Modules, not Monoliths.

Businesses are different.

A machine shop shouldn't see Salon features.

A water company shouldn't see Restaurant tables.

Every business only enables what it needs.

Principle 4
Progressive Complexity.

A new business should feel comfortable within five minutes.

As the business grows,

Karkhana grows with it.

Not before.

Principle 5
Every click should create value.

No unnecessary screens.

No unnecessary forms.

No unnecessary confirmations.

Principle 6
Data should never be entered twice.

Everything should flow.

Target Customers

Initially, Karkhana will focus on service-based and project-based businesses where customer acquisition, quotations, execution, and after-sales service are core to daily operations.

Examples include:

Water heating & water treatment companies (e.g., Aqua Elite)
HVAC contractors
Solar installers
Interior design & fit-out firms
Electrical contractors
Plumbing businesses
Fabrication workshops
Precision machine shops
Civil contractors
Facility management companies

These industries share a similar operational lifecycle:

Lead → Site Visit → Quotation → Approval → Execution → Invoice → Warranty → Repeat Business

By focusing on this common workflow, Karkhana can provide industry-specific experiences without fragmenting the core product.

The North Star

Every feature proposed for Karkhana must answer at least one of these questions:

Does it help the business earn more revenue?
Does it save the business owner time?
Does it improve the customer experience?
Does it make the business easier to operate?
Does it strengthen long-term customer retention?

If the answer to all five is no, the feature should not be built.

Final Thought for Volume I

Karkhana is not trying to replace every tool a business uses.

It is trying to become the place where a business begins its day and ends its day.

If a business owner logs into Karkhana every morning to see what needs attention—and relies on it to manage customers, work, and growth—then Karkhana has achieved its purpose
