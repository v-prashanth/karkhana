## My suggestion before Volume VII

After reading all six volumes, I think Karkhana is evolving into something much bigger than a CRM or operations tool. I would adjust the long-term roadmap slightly:

1. **Volumes I–VI** establish the product vision and architecture.
2. **Volume VII** should define **Modules & Capabilities**—every module (CRM, Website, Finance, Staff, Network, AI, etc.), what it does, and how it interacts with other modules.
3. **Volume VIII** should cover **Authentication, Authorization & Security**, since that's a cross-cutting concern affecting every module.

That order mirrors how mature SaaS platforms are typically designed and will make the blueprint much easier to maintain as Karkhana grows.
