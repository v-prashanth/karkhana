## Why CRM should come next

The Website's only purpose is to generate leads.

The moment someone submits a form, the Website Module hands the lead to the CRM.

So the next volume naturally answers:

> What happens after someone becomes a lead?

For Aqua Elite, for example:

```text
Google Search

↓

Website

↓

Lead

↓

Sales Follow-up

↓

Site Visit

↓

Quotation

↓

Negotiation

↓

Installation

↓

Invoice

↓

Payment

↓

Warranty

↓

AMC

↓

Referral

↓

Repeat Customer
```

This entire journey belongs to the CRM.

---

## My recommendation

I would rename the next volume to:

> **Volume VIII — CRM & Customer Lifecycle**

because it follows the customer's journey naturally.

It also makes the entire blueprint read like a story:

* Vision
* Product
* Architecture
* Database
* APIs
* Workspace
* Website
* **CRM**
* Operations
* Finance
* Growth
* Network
* AI

That progression is much more coherent than having two consecutive Website volumes.

I genuinely think this ordering will make the Karkhana blueprint feel like something written by the product and engineering leadership of a mature SaaS company rather than a collection of independent documents.
