I actually wouldn't call it just **"CRM."**

One of the goals we've discussed throughout Karkhana is **avoiding enterprise jargon**.

When people hear **CRM**, they immediately think:

* Salesforce
* Zoho CRM
* HubSpot

Karkhana should feel different.

Internally, you can absolutely call the module **CRM**, but the blueprint should define it as something much broader.

I'd title it:

> **Volume IX — Customer Lifecycle Management (CRM)**

This better reflects Karkhana's philosophy: it's not just storing contacts; it's managing the entire journey from first enquiry to repeat business.
