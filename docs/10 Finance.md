I think **Finance** is where Karkhana can become significantly different from most SMB software.

Most finance modules are built **for accountants**.

Karkhana's Finance module should be built **for business owners**.

A plumber, machine shop owner, solar installer, water heater dealer, or interior designer shouldn't need accounting knowledge to understand whether they're making money.

So this volume should focus on **cash flow, revenue, profitability, and business health**, while still supporting GST, invoices, and bookkeeping.

# Executive Philosophy

Every business asks the same questions.

> How much money did we make?

> How much money are customers yet to pay?

> How much did we spend?

> Are we actually profitable?

Most accounting software answers these questions only after navigating dozens of reports.

Karkhana should answer them immediately.

Finance is not about accounting.

Finance is about understanding the health of a business.

# Closing Statement

Finance is not just about recording transactions—it is about helping businesses make better decisions.

In Karkhana, every quotation, project, invoice, and payment contributes to a real-time understanding of business health. By tightly integrating Finance with CRM, Operations, and the Website Module, business owners no longer need to reconcile disconnected systems. Instead, they gain a single, accurate view of how work translates into revenue, profit, and long-term growth.

The ultimate goal of the Finance module is simple: **help business owners spend less time managing numbers and more time growing their business.**
