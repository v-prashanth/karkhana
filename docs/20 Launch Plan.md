# Launch Checklist

Before launch, verify:

* Product is feature-complete for the intended release.
* Security review completed.
* Performance targets met.
* Documentation published.
* Pricing finalized.
* Support team trained.
* Marketing assets ready.
* Website live.
* Analytics configured.
* Backup and rollback procedures verified.
* Customer onboarding tested end-to-end.
* Legal documents (Terms, Privacy Policy) published.

A launch is complete only when customers can discover, purchase, onboard, and successfully use the product without engineering intervention.
